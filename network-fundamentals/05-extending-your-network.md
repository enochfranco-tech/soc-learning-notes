## What is Port Forwarding?
In networking, port forwarding tells your router: “When traffic comes on this port → send it to this device.”

Real‑world example:

You host a Minecraft server on your PC.

You set port forwarding: “All visitors on port 25565 → go to my PC.”

Now your friends outside can join your game.

## What is Firewall?
A firewall is like a security guard at the gate of your network.

It checks every visitor (data packet) and decides: Allow, Block, or Record.

Real‑world example:

Imagine your office building in Bengaluru.

The guard checks IDs before letting people in.

Known employees = allowed, strangers = blocked, suspicious visitors = logged.

## What is VPN(Virtual Private Network)?
A VPN is like building a secret tunnel under a busy highway. Only you can use it, and nobody outside can see what’s inside.

Real‑world example:

Two offices, one in Bengaluru and one in Chennai.

Normally, they’re separate. But with a VPN, they connect through a secure tunnel over the Internet.

Employees can share files as if they’re in the same building.
