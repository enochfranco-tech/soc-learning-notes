## What this room covers
Hey people this room introduces the basics of computer networking, explaining how devices connect and share data.

## What I learned
What is Networking?
Networking is the practice of connecting two or more devices so they can share information or resources for a useful purpose.

What is internet?
The internet consists of many networks. To access any website or app, we need the internet because every website has a server, and that server is connected to the internet — a huge collection of interconnected networks. In simple words, the internet is the connection of many networks.

What is IP Address?
An Internet Protocol (IP) address is a numerical label assigned to a device connected to a network that uses the Internet Protocol for communication.
In simple term an IP address is like a pen name — it identifies a device, but it can change over time, just like an author might use different pen names.
An IP address has two types: IPv4 and IPv6. IPv4 is exhausting nowadays, so modern networks have started using IPv6.

## Why it matters for a SOC analyst
Understanding networking is the foundation for SOC analysts to detect abnormal traffic and identify potential attacks.
