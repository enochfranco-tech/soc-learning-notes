## What this room covers
This room introduces the basics of computer networking, explaining how devices connect and share data.


## What I learned
- Networking connects devices to share resources.  
- IP addresses identify devices uniquely.  
- Data travels in packets across networks.


## Key terms / commands
| Term       | What it means                          |
|------------|----------------------------------------|
| IP Address | Unique identifier for a device         |
| Packet     | Small unit of data sent over a network |
| LAN        | Local Area Network — connects devices  |

## Why it matters for a SOC analyst
Understanding networking basics helps SOC analysts detect abnormal traffic and identify potential attacks.
